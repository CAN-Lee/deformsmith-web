OVERVIEW

Generating Deformable Assets for Robot Manipulation

Text and images tell us what an object looks like. Interaction reveals how it behaves.

DeformSmith constructs deformable assets from text or a single image. A hierarchy of agents builds geometry, establishes physical models, configures material behavior, and plans robot interaction. A shared physics-grounded harness uses simulation probes and manipulation feedback to guide construction and refinement.

The outputs bring together geometry, appearance, physical parameters, validation records, and replayable interaction data in a common asset representation.

THE METHOD

Four layers. One feedback loop.

Progressively construct each part of an asset, then examine its behavior through simulation.

L0

Geometry

Reconstruct and align meshes, particles, and Gaussian appearance.

L1

Physical modeling

Establish mass, volume, contact conditions, and the initial state.

L2

Material behavior

Evaluate material candidates with deformation and recovery probes.

L3

Robot interaction

Use pick-and-place feedback to revise actions and material choices.
